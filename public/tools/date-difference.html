Date Difference Calculator — Free Browser Tool

Choose a start date and an end date.

Inputs are processed in this tab; this tool does not upload or save them.

Introduction

The date difference calculator measures a calendar span in days, full weeks and remaining days. It helps students and planners compare deadlines without converting months into guessed lengths.

How to Use

  1. Choose a start date and an end date.
  2. Enable inclusive counting if you want both endpoints counted.
  3. Select Calculate difference.
  4. Read the day total and its equivalent in full weeks plus days.

Why Use This Tool

  • Calendar-day arithmetic avoids daylight-saving hour differences.
  • Free to use, with no signup.
  • Entered data is processed locally without application storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this count business days?

No. All calendar days count, including weekends and holidays. The tool does not use a regional holiday calendar.

Why does inclusive counting add one day?

Standard subtraction makes identical dates zero days apart. Inclusive counting counts both endpoints, so identical dates count as one day.

Are my selected dates recorded?

No. The dates are used locally for the calculation and are not uploaded or saved by Kalika.
