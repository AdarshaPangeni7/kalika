Delete PDF Pages Online

Remove an unwanted cover, blank sheet or extra page. Preview the pages you keep before creating a separate PDF.

Your document stays on this device. No account or file upload needed. How privacy works · All PDF tools

One PDF · up to 30 MB · 1–100 pages. Choosing another file replaces the current project after it loads successfully.

Use an unlocked, flattened and unsigned PDF. Text stays as text; bookmarks and document-level features may not carry over. Review the download before sharing.

Choose a PDF to see its pages.

Select unwanted pages and choose Remove selected. The remaining thumbnails are the pages that will be exported. Keep at least one page.

    How to use

    1. Choose an unlocked PDF and identify the unwanted pages in the previews.
    2. Select those pages and choose Remove selected, or remove them individually.
    3. Check the remaining pages. Undo last change restores an accidental removal.
    4. Create the new PDF, then download and review it.

    Keep control of your document

    No signup. Processing happens locally in this browser tab. Download and inspect your result before closing the page.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I recover an accidentally deleted page?

    Use Undo last change, which keeps up to 30 recent editing steps. Restore original pages resets the document to its loaded state.

    Can I remove every page?

    You must keep at least one page to export a valid document. If you remove all pages, undo the removal or restore the original pages.

    Is deleting a page the same as redacting information?

    No. This tool removes pages from the output; it does not securely redact information elsewhere in the document. Do not use it as a redaction tool.

    Are my documents uploaded or saved?

    No. Your file is processed in the browser tab without a document upload or application storage. Website analytics are separate from file processing; see the Privacy page.

    Which documents can I use?

    Choose one unlocked PDF up to 30 MB and 100 pages. Form and signature fields are not supported. Bookmarks, attachments and accessibility structure may not transfer; review the output.
