Introduction

Use the BS to AD date converter to compare a Nepali birth date, record or appointment with its Gregorian equivalent. You can also convert AD to BS and browse the surrounding Nepali month without leaving the page.

How to Use

  1. Choose AD to BS or BS to AD.
  2. Enter an AD date, or select the BS year, month and day.
  3. Select Convert date to see both dates and the weekday.
  4. Browse adjacent BS months or choose Today in Nepal.

Why Use This Tool

  • Both conversion directions on one page.
  • Nepali month names and a calendar showing both date systems.
  • Free to use without signup; dates are processed locally.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the BS to AD date converter work?

It counts calendar days using bundled month-length data for BS 1975–2099. A fixed difference in years is not sufficient because the month lengths vary.

What is the supported date range?

The page displays the exact supported endpoints above the inputs. Dates outside that range are rejected rather than estimated. Future dates depend on the bundled calendar table; compare important dates with the relevant published calendar.

Does the calendar show festivals or holidays?

This calendar shows dates and weekdays only. It does not calculate festivals, lunar observances or public holidays.

Is my birth date uploaded or saved?

No. The conversion runs in your browser using calendar data served with this page. Kalika does not upload or save entered dates.

Which timezone does Today in Nepal use?

The shortcut takes today’s date in Asia/Kathmandu. Manually entered dates are calendar dates, so converting them does not shift the result based on your device’s timezone.
