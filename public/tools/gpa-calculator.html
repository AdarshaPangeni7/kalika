GPA Calculator — Free Browser Tool

Set the maximum grade-point scale used by your school.

Inputs are processed in this tab; this tool does not upload or save them.

Introduction

Use this GPA calculator online to combine subject grade points and credit hours into a weighted average. Students can check a term estimate using their institution’s numeric grade-point values.

How to Use

  1. Set the maximum grade-point scale used by your school.
  2. Enter each subject’s numeric grade points and credit hours.
  3. Use Add row for additional subjects; leave unused rows blank.
  4. Select Calculate GPA and compare the estimate with your school’s rules.

Why Use This Tool

  • Credit weighting is shown alongside the result.
  • Free to use, with no signup.
  • Entered data is processed locally without application storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the GPA calculated?

Each grade-point value is multiplied by its credit hours. The sum is divided by total credits; displayed results are rounded to four decimal places.

Can I enter letter grades or repeated courses?

Convert letter grades using your school’s official scale first. This tool does not apply repeat-course, pass/fail, honors or institutional rounding policies.

Does my school receive the grades I enter?

No. The calculator sends no grades to a school or server, and does not save a student record.
