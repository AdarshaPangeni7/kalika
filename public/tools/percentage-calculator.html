Introduction

This percentage calculator online covers three common questions: a percentage of a number, one value as a percentage of another, and change from an original value. Use it to check marks, quantities or everyday comparisons.

How to Use

  1. Choose the calculation mode.
  2. Enter the two values using the field labels and mode formula.
  3. Select Calculate percentage.
  4. Read the result and adjust the inputs to compare another example.

Why Use This Tool

  • Three percentage formulas are available without switching pages.
  • Free to use, with no signup.
  • Entered data is processed locally without application storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which number goes in each field?

In “A% of B”, A is the rate and B is the whole. In “A as % of B”, A is the part and B the whole. For change, A is the original and B is the new value.

How are zero and negative values handled?

A zero whole cannot be used for a share, and change from zero is undefined. Change uses (B − A) ÷ |A| × 100, so review whether that convention suits comparisons involving negative values.

Are my calculation inputs kept anywhere?

Kalika computes the answer in this tab and does not upload or save the numbers.
