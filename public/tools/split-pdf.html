Split PDF Online for Free

Keep only the pages you need from a longer document, or separate every page into its own PDF. Enter page ranges for a custom selection and download the result directly.

One PDF up to 30 MB and 200 pages. Separate-page downloads are limited to 100 MB combined.

    Use ordinary, unlocked PDFs. Forms and signature dictionaries are not supported. Your source files remain unchanged.

    Introduction

    Keep only the pages you need from a longer document, or separate every page into its own PDF. Enter page ranges for a custom selection and download the result directly.

    The tool runs on your device with no signup. Download the result when it is ready; choosing a file does not upload it to Kalika.

    How to Use

    1. Choose the PDF you want to split.
    2. Choose selected pages or a separate PDF for every page.
    3. For selected pages, enter numbers or ranges such as 1-3, 5.
    4. Select Split PDF, then download the PDF or ZIP. Extract the ZIP to access individual PDFs.

    Why Use This Tool

    • Extract a range without converting text to images.
    • Choose the order of selected pages.
    • Download all individual pages together as one ZIP.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I extract only a few pages?

    Choose Selected pages in one PDF and enter page numbers separated by commas. For example, 1-3, 5 extracts the first three pages and page five. Use the actual PDF page positions, not printed page labels.

    Can I download each page separately?

    Choose Every page as a separate PDF. The tool creates a ZIP containing files named page-001.pdf, page-002.pdf and so on. Extract the ZIP after downloading.

    Does splitting keep my PDF private?

    The selected file is processed in your browser. This tool does not upload the PDF or keep a saved history of the extracted pages.

    What types of PDFs cannot be split here?

    Password-protected files, damaged PDFs and documents containing form or signature dictionaries are not supported. Bookmarks, attachments and links to removed pages may not survive extraction.
