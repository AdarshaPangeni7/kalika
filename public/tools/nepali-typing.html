Roman letters to Nepali Unicode

This Nepali typing online tool helps you write messages, school notes and everyday documents using familiar Roman letters. It produces Unicode text that you can edit, copy and paste into apps that support Devanagari.

Common spellings receive dictionary suggestions; other words use the phonetic rules below. Transliteration preserves sounds rather than translating meaning. You stay in control of the final spelling.

How to use

  1. Keep Roman → Nepali selected and type a Nepali word in Roman letters.
  2. Press Space or Enter, or choose a spelling button beneath the editor.
  3. For a pasted paragraph, click Convert text. Select a smaller passage first if you want to preserve the rest.
  4. Correct spelling directly or insert characters from the keyboard.
  5. Copy the result or download a UTF-8 text file before leaving the page.

Why use this tool?

  • Text conversion runs locally without a signup.
  • English mode supports mixed-language writing.
  • Undo and Redo let you recover edits and cleared text.
  • The character keyboard gives direct access to letters and vowel marks.

Phonetic typing guide

Vowels change form after a consonant: kaa gives का, while aa gives आ. A single a gives the inherent vowel, as in ka → क. Capital T, D and N select retroflex letters; use lowercase for ordinary typing. Common-word suggestions can override these literal rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an English-to-Nepali translator?

No. Write Nepali sounds with Roman letters, such as mero naam, to get मेरो नाम. English sentences are not translated. Switch to English / direct typing to keep English words unchanged.

Can I type any Nepali word?

The tool combines a common-word dictionary with phonetic letter rules. Roman spellings are ambiguous, so names and less common words may need correction. Use the spelling choices, character keyboard or a Nepali keyboard on your device to edit the result. It is not a contextual AI spellchecker.

Is my Nepali text uploaded or saved?

Typing and conversion happen locally. This tool does not upload your text or save it in browser storage. Copy or download important writing before closing or reloading the page. Optional site analytics does not receive editor contents.

How do I keep English words, links and email addresses?

Choose English / direct typing before writing English text. Convert selected text changes only your selection; without a selection it converts all Roman words. The Convert button preserves web links and email addresses, along with existing Nepali text, punctuation and numbers.

Can I use this on my phone or without an internet connection?

The editor works with touch keyboards and an on-screen Nepali character keyboard. After the page has fully loaded, conversion needs no network requests. Opening or reloading the website still requires a connection.

How do I undo a conversion or type a half letter?

Use Undo or Ctrl+Z (Command+Z on Mac); Redo restores the undone step. Add _ after a consonant to request a halant, such as k_ for क्, or insert the ् character from the keyboard. The example button inserts at the cursor and can also be undone.
