Age Calculator — Free Browser Tool

Enter the date of birth.

Uses calendar dates in your local time zone. For a missing anniversary day, the last day of that month is used.

Introduction

Use this age calculator online to measure the time between a birth date and a comparison date. It displays completed years and months with remaining days, plus a total day count.

How to Use

  1. Enter the date of birth.
  2. Keep today’s comparison date or choose another date.
  3. Select Calculate age to view the calendar breakdown.

Why Use This Tool

  • A comparison date supports past and future calculations.
  • Free to use, with no signup.
  • Entered data is processed locally without application storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are month-end birthdays handled?

Month additions clamp the day to the final day of a shorter month. This makes a January 31 start reach a full month on the final day of February.

Is this an official eligibility or legal-age check?

No. It uses calendar dates and a month-end convention, not institution-specific eligibility rules, birth times or local legal definitions. Use valid dates from years 1000 through 9999.

Does Kalika retain my date of birth?

No. The date is used within this tab for the calculation; the tool does not upload it or create a stored profile.
