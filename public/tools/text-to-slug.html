Text to Slug Converter — Free Browser Tool

Paste a title or short phrase.

Up to 200,000 characters. Inputs are processed in this tab; this tool does not upload or save them.

Introduction

The text to slug converter turns a title into a lowercase path segment for a page or article. It replaces punctuation and spaces with hyphens while retaining letters and numbers from multiple writing systems.

How to Use

  1. Paste a title or short phrase.
  2. Select Create slug.
  3. Check the generated path segment for clarity.
  4. Copy the slug into your website editor.

Why Use This Tool

  • Repeated separators become a single hyphen.
  • Free to use, with no signup.
  • Entered data is processed locally without application storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Chinese and Hindi characters supported?

Unicode letters and numbers are retained rather than transliterated. Accent marks following Latin letters are removed; marks in other writing systems are retained. Review the slug before publishing.

Will this create a unique working URL?

No. It creates a path segment only. You must check your website for duplicate slugs and create the corresponding page or redirect.

Are my proposed page titles saved?

No. Slugs are generated in the browser tab without sending the title to a server or storing it in Kalika.
