Grade Calculator — Free Browser Tool

Enter each completed assessment’s score as a percentage.

Inputs are processed in this tab; this tool does not upload or save them.

Introduction

The weighted grade calculator combines percentage scores for exams, assignments and other assessments. It shows both the average of entered work and the points that work contributes toward the full course.

How to Use

  1. Enter each completed assessment’s score as a percentage.
  2. Enter its weight in the full course as a percentage.
  3. Add rows as needed; keep the total weight at or below 100%.
  4. Select Calculate grade to see the weighted average and final-grade contribution.

Why Use This Tool

  • Separates completed-work average from final-grade contribution.
  • Free to use, with no signup.
  • Entered data is processed locally without application storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when my weights total less than 100%?

The weighted average uses only entered assessments. For example, 80% on work worth 50% of the course gives an 80% completed-work average and contributes 40 percentage points to the final grade.

Will the result match my official course grade?

Only if the entered scores and weights match its rules. Dropped assessments, extra credit, grade curves and letter-grade boundaries are not applied.

Are assessment scores saved in a profile?

No. There is no student profile, and these scores are processed in your browser without upload or application storage.
