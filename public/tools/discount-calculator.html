Calculate Sale Prices and Discount Savings

Check what you will pay after a sale discount. Add a second percentage discount or a fixed reduction, then see the final price, total saving and each calculation step.

Check the promotion terms before combining discounts. Tax and shipping are not added by this calculator.

How to Use

  1. Enter the original listed price and choose a display currency.
  2. Enter the first percentage discount.
  3. Optionally add a second percentage discount and a fixed reduction.
  4. Select Calculate savings to see the final price and breakdown.
  5. Copy the summary if you want to keep the calculation.

Why Use This Tool

  • See the effect of sequential percentage discounts.
  • Include a fixed reduction after percentage discounts.
  • Read a breakdown with amounts rounded to cents.
  • Calculate locally without signup or saving a price history.

How this discount calculator works

A percentage discount reduces the price by the rate you enter. A second discount applies to the remaining price, not the original amount. For example, 20% off 100.00 leaves 80.00; another 10% removes 8.00, leaving 72.00. A further fixed reduction of 5.00 gives a final price of 67.00 and a total saving of 33.00.

Amounts use two decimal places. Calculations use integer arithmetic and round half a cent upward. The result is a calculation from your inputs, not a verified merchant quote or invoice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are two discounts added together?

No. They are applied in sequence. A 20% discount followed by 10% gives a 28% saving before rounding, not 30%. The breakdown shows the amount removed at each step.

Where does a fixed reduction apply?

The fixed reduction is subtracted after both percentage discounts. It cannot exceed the remaining price. Store promotions may use a different order or restrict combinations, so enter only the discounts that apply to your purchase.

Does this add tax or delivery charges?

No. It discounts the listed price you enter. If that price already includes tax, the discounted price remains on the same basis; the tool does not separately allocate tax. Use Sales Tax & VAT Calculator for a separate tax breakdown.

How precise are the results?

Prices and fixed reductions support two decimal places up to 1,000,000. Discount rates support up to four decimal places, from 0 to 100%. Each discount amount is rounded to the nearest cent, with half a cent rounded up. The effective saving is displayed to two decimal places.

Are my shopping amounts stored or sent elsewhere?

No. The discount calculator processes inputs in this browser tab and does not upload them or save calculation history. Copy summary uses your clipboard only when you press it. Currency selection changes formatting, not exchange rates.
