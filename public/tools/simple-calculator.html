Introduction

This simple calculator online handles basic arithmetic for everyday totals, quantities and study exercises. Use the on-screen buttons or your keyboard to work with whole numbers, decimals and negative values.

How to Use

  1. Enter your first number using the buttons or keyboard.
  2. Select add, subtract, multiply or divide.
  3. Enter the next number and press equals to see the result.
  4. Continue with another operation, or select AC to start again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculator follow the order of operations?

It works like a basic pocket calculator, applying operations from left to right as you select them. For example, 2 + 3 × 4 gives 20. It does not support parentheses or scientific expressions.

How does the percent button work?

The % button divides the displayed number by 100: 15 becomes 0.15. To find 15% of 200, enter 200 × 15 % =. For a percentage increase or decrease, use our Percentage Calculator.

How precise are the answers?

You can enter up to 12 digits per number. Results are rounded to 12 significant digits after each operation, and very large or small values may use scientific notation. Repeating decimals are approximate; this is not an arbitrary-precision calculator.

Are my numbers uploaded or saved?

Calculations run in this browser tab. The calculator does not upload your numbers or save calculation history. Refreshing or closing the page clears the calculation.

Can I use a keyboard or fix a mistake?

Focus the calculator and use number keys, decimal point, +, −, * and /. Press Enter or = for the result, Backspace to remove the last entered digit, and Escape to clear. The ± button changes the displayed number’s sign.
