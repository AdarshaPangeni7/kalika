    Build a PDF from photos and scanned pages

    Combine your photos into one PDF, keeping the whole image by default. For photographed documents, use the scanner controls to find borders, correct perspective and improve readability before exporting.

    JPG to PDF supports ordinary pictures and paper photographs in the same project. Use original color for pictures; try the document filters for notes or receipts. Free rotation adds white corners so the image is not clipped. Auto-enhance may change colors or erase faint marks; compare the preview with Original color.

    How to Use

    1. Add photos or take a picture on a supported phone.
    2. Use the full image or select Detect document. Drag any corner to adjust the crop.
    3. Choose a filter and rotation, then Apply changes.
    4. Add more photos, edit individual pages and change their order.
    5. Select your page size, create the PDF or JPG, then use its download link.

    Why Use This Tool

    • Process photos locally without signing up.

    • Reorder pages before combining them into a PDF.
    • Export a single page as PDF or JPG.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Will this converter crop my pictures automatically?

    No. JPG to PDF keeps the full image when you add it. Choose Detect document for a paper photo, or position the four crop corners yourself. Apply changes to preview the result.

    Can I rearrange or remove images before making the PDF?

    Yes. Each page has Edit, Move up, Move down and Remove controls. The final PDF follows the page list from top to bottom. You can add more images to the same project.

    Which page sizes and image formats are available?

    Upload JPG, PNG or WebP photos. Export on A4 or US Letter pages with a 10 mm margin, or fit the PDF page to the image. Transparent areas become white. Images are encoded as JPEG and capped at 2,200 pixels on the longest side.

    Are the photos sent to Kalika?

    No. The converter reads photos and creates the PDF in this browser tab. It does not upload document images or store a scan history. Save your download before leaving the page.

    Will fine print stay exactly like the original?

    Exports are image-based and use JPEG compression. Resizing or enhancement can soften fine details, and no OCR text layer is added. Review the preview and downloaded PDF before sharing important documents. Use up to 20 photos, 20 MB each, 60 MB combined and 24 megapixels per photo.
