Kalika privacy policy

How Kalika handles files, inputs and connections.

Updated September 16, 2026.

Your files stay in your browser

Images and PDFs are processed entirely in your browser using JavaScript and canvas. Kalika does not upload those files to a server or store them on a Kalika server. Text, QR content and calculator inputs remain in the current tab. The tools do not save them in browser storage.

No Kalika account required

No account, sign-up or login is needed to use Kalika’s tools. The tools do not ask for your name or email. Analytics is optional and stays off unless you choose Allow analytics. No advertising scripts are enabled.

Optional Google Analytics

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics receives page-visit and basic browser/device information and uses analytics cookies. Kalika does not send your files, filenames, tool inputs, URL query strings or fragments to Analytics. Enhanced measurement and advertising personalization are disabled. Google receives ordinary connection information when your browser contacts its service. Read Google’s data-use information.

You can reject analytics and still use every tool. Use Privacy choices in the footer to change your decision. Your choice is saved in this browser’s local storage. Withdrawing consent stops future collection and removes Kalika’s Analytics cookies; it does not delete data already received by Google.

Connections needed to run the site

Your browser requests pages from the hosting provider, fonts served with the site, and tool libraries from jsDelivr only for tools that need them. Those services receive ordinary connection data such as an IP address and browser headers. They do not receive your selected files or tool text from Kalika. Hosting providers may retain operational or security logs under their own policies.

The currency converter requests currency pairs from Frankfurter. It sends currency codes, not your entered amount. If Frankfurter is unavailable, the converter requests rates from ExchangeRate-API using only the source currency code. Conversion is calculated locally. Both services receive standard network information. Previously retrieved rates are held only in tab memory; backup responses are reused for up to an hour. Nothing is written to browser storage.

Email is optional

If you contact the operator at cr08320@gmail.com, your email address and the message are received for responding to your request. Do not send sensitive files. Email is not required for any tool.

Advertising

No ads currently run on Kalika. If Google AdSense is introduced, this policy will be updated before activation and any required consent controls will be added. Google advertising services may use cookies; read how Google uses information from partner sites.

Questions

Contact the operator at cr08320@gmail.com with privacy questions.
