About Kalika

A small collection of tools for everyday tasks.

Kalika is an independently operated collection of free browser tools. It was made for the small tasks that interrupt a day: getting an image to the right size, turning a document into pictures, checking a number or counting words.

A practical workbench

Open a tool, do the task and take the result with you. There are no Kalika accounts, subscriptions or installations. The tools use your browser to process your files, so you keep control of the originals and the results.

Made to be straightforward

Each tool explains its limits and shows an error when it cannot complete a task. Calculators provide estimates and file tools keep the originals unchanged. Keeping a backup is always a good idea.
