About Kalika

I’m the creator of Kalika, based in Nepal, and I use Kalika as my public name. I built it so people could find useful everyday tools in one place without having to hand over their documents, photos or text just to get a small task done.

Useful tools, without an account

Kalika brings together PDF tools, image tools, calculators and text utilities. You can open a tool, complete a task and take the result with you. There is no Kalika account to create and no software to install.

Your files stay in your browser

Kalika's file and text tools process their inputs on your device rather than uploading them to a processing server. Keeping that work in the browser is central to why I built the site. Download the result you need and keep your original files.

That does not mean visiting the website creates no network activity. Cloudflare hosts and delivers the site. Google Analytics starts only if you allow it through Privacy choices. Some features, such as currency conversion, fetch external information. Read the Privacy page for details about the site's services and analytics.

Clear limits and room to improve

These tools are intended to make everyday tasks easier. Each tool explains its limits, and you should check downloaded files and calculation results before relying on them. If something does not work as expected, I want to hear about it.

You can contact me at hello@kalikatools.com with a problem report or suggestion. Please describe what happened without sending sensitive documents or personal information.
